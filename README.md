# Queensland Time Travel

A choose-your-path time-travel web app by Robin McKean. It joins four Queensland eras (the grass rush, the Native Mounted Police, the gold rushes and the sugar industry) into one journey. It goes with the book *Walking on Ancient Ground (Time Travel on Country, A Choose Your Own Character Adventure)*.

- Student version: https://robmac10.github.io/queensland-time-travel/
- Teacher version (discussion answers and scene notes behind "Show" buttons; not linked from the student version, set to noindex): https://robmac10.github.io/queensland-time-travel/teacher/

## How it works

1. **Hub.** A photo hero, a timeline of the four eras (1840 to 1910) and four stops. You can go in order or choose any era.
2. **Visit an era.** An arrival page gives the content warning and a Real history panel, then you take the place of an invented person and choose a path. Every scene has a "Real history" panel with sources.
3. **Return.** Each ending says "Your visit is over", then shows **What really happened** with its sources and a link to the era's live information bank. You can return to the hub or go to the next era.
4. **Back to Country.** When you have reached an ending in all four eras (tracked in your browser's localStorage), a short reflection opens.

Each era also has its own Behind the Story page (everything invented), Discuss and write page, and Sources page. A Home button at the end of every page links to https://robmac10.github.io/history.html.

## The four eras

| Stop | Era | Information bank (live) | People you can be (all invented) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The Grass Rush, 1840 to 1902 | https://robmac10.github.io/grass-rush-adventure/ | Will (1846), Alex (1869), an unnamed Aboriginal person (1840) |
| 2 | The Native Police, 1849 to 1904 | https://robmac10.github.io/native-police-adventure/ | Billy (late 1870s) |
| 3 | The Gold Rush, 1858 to 1877 | https://robmac10.github.io/qld-gold-rush-adventure/ | Sam (1858), Lin (1874), an unnamed Aboriginal person (1873) |
| 4 | The Sugar Story, 1863 to 1908 | https://robmac10.github.io/sugar-story-adventure/ | Harry (1884) |

The stories, Real history panels, sources, Behind the Story text and discussion questions are the archived story versions of the four information-bank projects (git tag `story-version`, folder `archive/story-version/`), imported unchanged by `tools/import-eras.js`.

## Content warning and review

This app may contain names and images of deceased Aboriginal, Torres Strait Islander and South Sea Islander people. It has **not yet been reviewed** by Traditional Owners or by Australian South Sea Islander community groups (for example QUASSIC). Please seek that review before using it widely. There are no sacred stories. The Aboriginal and South Sea Islander characters have no names, language groups or customs on purpose.

## Building

Edit the files in `src/` and run:

    node build.js

This writes the student version to the repository root and the teacher version to `teacher/`. Do not edit the built files in the root or in `teacher/`. The build also checks that every source key, scene link and image key exists, and that every scene has a Real history panel.

- `src/framing.js`: the hub text, era cards, arrival text, "Your visit is over" message, Back to Country text, and the teacher notes and cross-era questions. This is the only new text in the app.
- `src/eras/*.json`: the four stories (generated by `tools/import-eras.js` from the archived story versions).
- `src/app.js`: the app. Blocks between `/*T*/` and `/*/T*/` are teacher-only.
- `src/index.html`, `src/styles.css`: page and style. Blocks between `<!--T-->` and `<!--/T-->` are teacher-only.
- `images/`: public domain photographs and maps, each credited on the page where it appears and on the About page.
- `tools/import-eras.js`: one-off importer (needs the four repos next to this one).

The student build has no teacher notes or answers anywhere in its code.

## Rules for this project

No invented historical facts. Invented people and events are labelled in Behind the Story. Public domain, credited images only, no AI images. Plain prose for teen readers, Australian English, always "path". No tracking and no cookies.
